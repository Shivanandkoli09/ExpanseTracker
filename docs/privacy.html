Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

In short: MyMoney collects only what it needs to run your account and store the expenses you enter. We don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell or share your data. You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains how MyMoney ("the app", "we", "us"), developed by Shivanand Koli, handles your information.

1. Information we collect

DataWhyWhere it's stored
Email address and passwordTo create and sign in to your account. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.Google Firebase
Your nameTo personalise the app.Google Firebase
Transactions you enter (title, amount, date, type, category)To show your expenses, sync them across devices and generate insights.Google Firebase and on your device
Saving goal (optional)To tailor your insights.Google Firebase
Category corrections and reminder settingsTo improve auto-categorisation and remind you at your chosen time.On your device only

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, bank account or card details, device advertising identifier, or any analytics or usage tracking data. MyMoney does not connect to your bank — it only stores what you type in.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: storing, syncing and categorising your transactions.
  • To generate spending insights. These are calculated by the app itself; your data is not sent to third-party AI services.
  • To send the daily reminder you can turn on or off in Profile. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • To respond to you when you contact support.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties.

3. Service providers

MyMoney uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to securely store your account and data. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms. Data may be stored on servers outside your country. See Firebase's privacy information.

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. Access to your data in the cloud is limited to your signed-in account. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, all your transactions and your profile from our cloud database and clears MyMoney's data from your device. Residual copies in our provider's backups are removed on the provider's standard schedule.

You can also request deletion by emailing us (see below).

6. Your rights

You can view and edit your information in the app, and delete it at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may also have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, or to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Children

MyMoney is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
