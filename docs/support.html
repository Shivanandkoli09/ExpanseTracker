Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at expansesupport@gmail.com. Including your iPhone model and the app version (Profile → Version) helps us help you faster.

Frequently asked questions

How does auto-categorisation work?

As you type a title like "Swiggy dinner" or "Uber to office", MyMoney suggests a category. If you change it, the app remembers your choice for similar transactions next time.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and we'll email you a reset link. If you're signed in, use Profile → Change Password.

Is my data synced across devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and your transactions sync automatically. You can also add transactions offline; they sync when you're back online.

How do I change or turn off the daily reminder?

Go to Profile → Reminders. If reminders don't arrive, check that notifications for MyMoney are allowed in iOS Settings.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account and all of your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
